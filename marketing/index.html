Features

Shape the itinerary

Add a stop, insert one before or after another, or drag activities into a new order. The timeline shifts with you. Tighten a day or add buffer when the gaps feel wrong.

Reservations and money

Store booking and confirmation details on each activity. Track estimated or actual cost per stop. See the running total against the plan budget so you know where you stand.

Real places and directions

Search with Google Places and drop a real location into the plan. The Place ID feeds Google Maps so anyone can get driving directions to the next stop.

Roles, invites, and linked plans

Invite Co-Planners and Travelers. Vote on ideas. When a Traveler needs their own timing, they link the plan and become the planner of their copy. Their party can join the trip late or leave early while the original plan stays intact.

Templates

Save a plan as a template. Publish it for others. Browse the Library for successfully rated templates and start from one instead of a blank page.

AI when you want it

Ask for a kid-friendly stop near the hotel and get a ready-to-apply suggestion with a real place. Use monthly credits or bring your own key.
